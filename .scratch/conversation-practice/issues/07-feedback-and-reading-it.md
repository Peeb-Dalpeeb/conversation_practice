# 07 — Feedback, and reading it after an Attempt

**What to build:** When judging finishes, the Trainee reads coaching prose about the Attempt
they just took — told what to do differently, not merely what they got wrong, and pointed at
specific moments in their own Attempt rather than at conversations in general. Written *to*
them, not about them: coaching, not a performance review.

Feedback is a second, separate call. It receives the Assessment *and* the Transcript, and is
instructed that the verdicts are fixed — its job is to explain and advise, not to re-open
them.

The separation is the point. A single call producing verdicts *and* prose will write "you
built great rapport!" directly above a scorecard showing four failures — the exact case of a
Trainee who is warm, courteous and professional but never asks what happened and scores 2 of
6. Leadership then believes one of the two and we cannot control which. Grade first, coach
second.

This replaces the Feedback stub from ticket 05. The persisted Attempt now holds all three:
Transcript, Assessment, and Feedback — which is what makes a tuning run that went wrong
readable afterwards instead of guessed at.

Getting the tone right is ticket 12's job. This ticket has to produce the Feedback, persist
it, and put it in front of the Trainee.
