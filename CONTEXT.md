# Conversation Practice

A tool for rehearsing difficult conversations out loud against an AI that plays the other
person, then reading back a judged account of how it went and trying again.

## Language

### Authored content

**Scenario**:
A complete practice setup: the situation, the constraints on the trainee, the persona, and
the rubric.
_Avoid_: case, exercise, simulation

**Persona**:
The character the AI plays, defined by a public description, a private profile, and its
behaviour rules.
_Avoid_: personality, character, bot, AI

**Private Profile**:
The facts about the persona the trainee cannot see and must uncover through the
conversation.
_Avoid_: backstory, secret, hidden state

**Gate**:
The named condition that must be met before the persona softens. Until it is met, the
persona stays guarded.
_Avoid_: trigger, unlock, win condition

**Rubric**:
The fixed set of criteria every attempt is judged against.
_Avoid_: scorecard, checklist, criteria list

**Briefing**:
What the trainee is told before an attempt begins: the situation and the limits of what
they are allowed to offer. Seen only beforehand, never during.
_Avoid_: instructions, setup, prompt, scenario description

### Practice and outcome

**Trainee**:
The person practising the conversation.
_Avoid_: employee, user, learner, student

**Attempt**:
A single spoken run of a scenario, from opening line to close.
_Avoid_: session, run, conversation, call, take

**Transcript**:
The turn-by-turn text of an attempt. A reconstruction of what was said, not a recording of
it — it can disagree with what the trainee remembers saying.
_Avoid_: recording, log, notes

**Assessment**:
The per-criterion judgment of an attempt: met or not met, each with a quoted line from the
transcript as evidence.
_Avoid_: grade, score, evaluation

**Feedback**:
The coaching written for the trainee to read after an attempt. Derived from the assessment.
_Avoid_: review, critique, notes
